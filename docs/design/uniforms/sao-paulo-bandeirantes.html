São Paulo Bandeirantes

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Yellow, blue and green are the same values the game uses for the São Paulo badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Passion One is a fat, condensed poster face, so a long word like BANDEIRANTES still fits.
  • Short words, wide margins. BANDEIRANTES at home, SÃO PAULO on the road, and a big SP on the alternate, all well inside the body.
  • Letters on the caps, the drawing on the patch. SP carries the caps, helmet and sleeve badge. The bandeira flag only appears on the patch, where it has room.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
