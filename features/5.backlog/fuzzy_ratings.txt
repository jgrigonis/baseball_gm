Although we are showing letter grades instead of 
exact numbers, we aren't fuzzing them.
In order to do so, we'd need a per-gm table of 
fuzzed ratings. 

One consideration is that human GMs could collaborate
to compare ratings and determine which are accurate.

Already an issue is that sorts seem to go off numbers 
rather than displayed letter grades, but I can't confirm this. 
The most obvious situation is with the draft, which displays
one order, but obviously with many tied ratings, but the draft 
takes place in another order. This is fine and expected as the 
computer can see the numbers, but the human GM is also getting 
the advantage of this without saving their preferences and 
leaving it as default.
