6-4-3 (Shortstop to Second to First): This is the most common double play, typically 
accounting for approximately 40% to 45% of all double plays turned.

4-6-3 (Second to Shortstop to First): The second most frequent type, comprising 
roughly 20% to 25% of double plays.

5-4-3 (Third to Second to First): This "around the horn" play accounts for 
about 10% to 15% of double plays.

Others: The remaining 15% to 30% include a wide variety of less common sequences such as:
3-6-3: Initiated by the first baseman.
1-6-3: Initiated by the pitcher.
Strikeout-Caught Stealing: A "strike-em-out, throw-em-out" double play.
Lineouts: Where a runner is doubled off a base after a catch.
Unassisted: Where a single fielder records both outs.
