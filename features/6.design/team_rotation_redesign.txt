team_rotations re-design

team_rotation_redesign.txt

UI gives a hint

Player presents: are my starter(s) in this order from 1 to N.
A glow green light highlighting who goes next.
At the bottom of the list is always a "drag player here" area similar to batting order 

User can delete pitchers from roation with a little x
This will leave a gap in the rotation and replace with a "drag player here" box
If the x'd out pitcher had the green light, it looks for a new place, moving down and back to 1.
If the x'd out player was the last in the rotation, the list shrinks leaving just 1 drag box at the bottom.

User can add to the rotation with a + on the pitchers in the bullpen
    or dragging to any "drag box"
by default the + will send them to the first open slot starting from the Top
    finally, it will just append them

Move pitchers around in the rotation with little carets, up or down
If you move a player with the green light, it follows him

The green light can be repositioned by simply clicking a black radio button making it glow green 

Nothing saves to the DB until the user presses save rotation

Some caveats: nothing stops the user from bing stupid.
They could fill their rotation with injured pitchers
They could have a 1 pitcher rotation 
They could have no rotation.

However, the green light should always end up on a player if there is one in the rotation.
It can't point to a drag slot

AI fills in the blanks

Now, at game time, the AI may have to help decide on the starting pitcher.
If the green light was pointing to an injured pitcher, there are a couple
logical options:
1. Skip that pitcher in the rotation (assuming there is anyone else in
the rotation)
2. Fill in with the best possible, healthy, non-fatigued bullpen pitcher who
isn't the closer, who hasn't pitched in N days.

In the AI Strategy panel the choice should toggle between 1 and 2.
If they choose Option 1, there should be a slider to indicate how short
you are willing to make the rotation from 1 to 5. So, if you put it on
5, but you have a 5 man rotation, any injury will then jump to option 2.
If you had it set to 3 with a 5 man rotation, you could continue rotating
with 2 injured pitchers sitting out.

Option 2 you set how many days rest you expect for your starter who is filling
in. The idea is this covers when your whole starting rotation is injured for
example, you don't want to crunsh your bullpen, you'd want to give them starter 
type rest intervals.

If no one meets the criteria for option 2 considering its N setting, you just go
with the best current SP rating (accounting for fatigue)

Players can also set their criteria for disqualifying a pitcher from starting.
Low energy pitchers will be treated like injured above. Options are on a slider:

    FRESH_THRESHOLD = 0.85
    GOOD_THRESHOLD = 0.65
    TIRED_THRESHOLD = 0.40
    EXHAUSTED_THRESHOLD = 0.01

But only the classes are shown as markers on the slider. The default is TIRED, and you can 
only move up to fresh, leaving some bar above that
