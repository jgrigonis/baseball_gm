Warsaw Hussars

The Winged Hussars, Poland's armored cavalry, rode with tall feathered wings on their backs. This set uses the white and red of the Polish flag with black trim. The lettering is a heavy Didone serif, the wing goes on the cap, and the patch is a red shield with the wing over two crossed lances.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Red, white and black are the same values the game uses for the Warsaw badge.
  • The flag, both ways. White over red at home, red with white trim on the road, and white over red again on every stirrup.
  • Didone capitals. A heavy, high-contrast serif, the look of old Polish posters and banners. All three jerseys arch the lettering, and the alternate uses the Polish name.
  • One symbol on the cap. The wing, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion and tower.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
