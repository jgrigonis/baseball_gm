Dubai Falcons

The desert and the falcon, the national bird. Sand gold like the dunes at dusk, deep slate like the glass towers at night, and palm green like an oasis. The lettering is Kufic, geometric like Arabic calligraphy carved in stone, and the patch reads دبي.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Sand gold, slate and green are the same values the game uses for the Dubai badge. I added an ivory for the home body, since none of the three is light enough, and a darker sand for the dune shadow.
  • Kufic lettering. Reem Kufi is drawn from geometric Kufic calligraphy and covers both English and Arabic, so دبي on the patch matches Falcons on the chest.
  • A 12° climb with a wing below. The wing sits fully under the word, following the Mumbai lesson.
  • One symbol on the cap. Like Mumbai's anchor and New York's spire, the cap has only the falcon.
  • Every word stays inside the seams. Measured against the jersey body.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
