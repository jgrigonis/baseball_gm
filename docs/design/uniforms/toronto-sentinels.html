Toronto Sentinels

The Needle stands watch over the city. Pine green and charcoal from the Canadian Shield, with a gold trim. "Sentinels" is in a classic ballpark script, and TORONTO is in a heavy slab serif. The cap carries the CN Tower, and the patch puts it over Lake Ontario.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Pine, charcoal and gold are the same values the game uses for the Toronto badge. I added a cream for the home body.
  • Why the Needle. A sentinel stands alone and keeps watch, and nothing in Toronto stands taller or watches over more than the CN Tower.
  • Script at home, block on the road. The script rises at 12°. TORONTO and the numbers use a heavy slab serif.
  • One symbol on the cap. The tower, following the anchor, spire, falcon and morion.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
