Uniform set · extra team

Moscow Cossacks

Malachite teal, Cossack gold and Kremlin red, the colors of the onion domes on Red Square. The logo is a Cossack in a black papakha fur hat with a big drooping moustache and the cartridge rows of his cherkesska coat. The lettering is Ruslan Display, drawn after old Russian sign lettering: COSSACKS on cream at home, MOSCOW on the teal road jersey, and a red Cherkesska alternate with МОСКВА in Cyrillic.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Malachite teal, Cossack gold and Kremlin red are the values the game uses for the Moscow badge: a teal badge with MOS in gold and a red ring.
  • Not another red team. Red and gold is already London, Beijing and Warsaw, so Moscow leads with teal, the green-blue of the malachite in the Kremlin's halls and of Saint Basil's domes. The nearest badges in the pool are Madrid and Cairo, and no other European team wears teal.
  • Display type. Ruslan Display is drawn after old Russian lettering and has a full Cyrillic set, so МОСКВА on the alternate is in the same face as COSSACKS.
  • The Cossack is the logo. The caps, helmet and sleeve badge carry a Cossack's head in a tall astrakhan papakha, with heavy brows, a drooping moustache and the gazyri cartridge tubes on his coat. The black of the hat is the only color outside the three, and it repeats on the stirrups and the alternate's belt.
  • In the game. Moscow is one of the extra teams, placed in Europe east of Warsaw by longitude.
