New York Titans

Art Deco Manhattan. Navy like the city at night, steel gray like the girders the ironworkers walked, and parchment like an old ticket stub. The lettering is tall and condensed like stamped steel, and the mark is a skyscraper spire with stacked arches and a needle on top.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Navy, steel and parchment are the same values the game uses for the New York badge, plus a lighter gray for the away pants.
  • Art Deco shapes, industrial letters. The spire, setback socks and sunburst patch are Deco; the lettering is Oswald, condensed like stamped steel, picked from a font study.
  • Straight lettering at home. Vienna and Mumbai climb and Singapore arches, so TITANS stands flat and square on its girder.
  • A symbol on the cap. Like Mumbai's anchor and Singapore's skyline, the cap carries the spire alone.
  • Every word stays inside the seams. Measured against the jersey body.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
