Milan Serpents

The biscione, the crowned serpent of old Milan, in olive green, silver and the red of the city's cross. The lettering is a Bodoni, the Didone of every Milanese fashion masthead. The cap carries a Bodoni S with fangs, the alternate has serpent scales across the hem, and the patch coils the serpent in front of the Duomo's spires.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Olive, silver and red are the same values the game uses for the Milan badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Fashion type. Bodoni Moda at its heaviest weight, the Didone of the Milanese fashion masthead.
  • Short words, wide margins. SERPENTS at home and on the alternate, and MILANO on the road, all well inside the body.
  • The biscione. A crowned serpent, the old emblem of the city, drawn here as a black cobra with a spiked hood, jaws open and fangs bared, without the figure in its jaws. It's on the patch, the caps and sleeves carry a fanged Bodoni S, and its scales run across the alternate hem and the stirrups.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
