Istanbul Sultans

Iznik tiles: cobalt, turquoise and coral red on white, the colors of the tiles that line the city's mosques and palaces. The lettering is Lalezar, a bold display face named for the tulip garden. The tulip, the emblem of the Ottoman court, goes on the cap, and the patch is a tile roundel with the skyline of domes and minarets.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Cobalt, turquoise and coral are the same values the game uses for the Istanbul badge, and they happen to be the three classic Iznik tile glazes. I added an ivory for the home body.
  • The tile band. Every sleeve and stirrup ends in a strip of tile border: a solid band with a row of coral dots.
  • Lalezar. A heavy display face with soft curves. Its name means "tulip garden".
  • One symbol on the cap. The tulip, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion, tower and wing.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
