| Outcome                | Runner Effect                                              | Batter Effect                | PA End? | Notes                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **single**             | Lead runner(s) advance 1 base; runner on 1B may score      | Batter to 1B                 | Yes     | Most common simple hit; can be combined with errors for multi-base advancement. |
| **double**             | Lead runner(s) advance 2 bases; runners on 2B/3B may score | Batter to 2B                 | Yes     |                                                                                 |
| **triple**             | All lead runners score except possibly 3B runner?          | Batter to 3B                 | Yes     | Rare, clears bases.                                                             |
| **home_run**           | All runners on bases score                                 | Batter scores                | Yes     | Clears bases; RBI credit for each runner.                                       |
| **ground_out**         | Runners may advance 1 base                                 | Batter out                   | Yes     | May generate `OutRecordedEvent`.                                                |
| **fly_out**            | Runners may tag and advance 1 base (sacrifice fly)         | Batter out                   | Yes     | Often 1 run scores from 3B.                                                     |
| **line_out**           | Usually no advancement                                     | Batter out                   | Yes     | Could be modeled as force out or error variant.                                 |
| **pop_out / foul_out** | Usually no advancement                                     | Batter out                   | Yes     |                                                                                 |
| **error**              | Batter reaches base                                        | Lead runners may advance     | Yes     | Can be combined with a hit to make “single + error” etc.                        |
| **double_play**        | Two outs recorded, some runners may advance                | Batter out                   | Yes     | Rare; counts as 2 outs.                                                         |
| **triple_play**        | Three outs recorded, runners may advance                   | Batter out                   | Yes     | Very rare; clears inning if applicable.                                         |
| **sacrifice_bunt**     | Runners advance 1 base                                     | Batter out                   | Yes     | Special case: batter intentionally out to advance runner.                       |
| **sacrifice_fly**      | Runner on 3B may score                                     | Batter out                   | Yes     | Only scores if less than 2 outs typically.                                      |
| **foul_ball_in_play**  | Usually no advancement                                     | Batter not out unless caught | Maybe   | Only ends PA if caught.                                                         |
| **fielders_choice**    | Lead runner is put out                                     | Batter to 1B                 | Yes     | If multiple baserunners, any one could be put out, others move                  |

Key Notes

Out events (OutRecordedEvent) are always tied to BIP that produces an out.
PA ends whenever the batter reaches base safely (single, double, triple, HR, walk, HBP) or is out (strikeout, ground_out, etc.).
Runners advance according to simple rules:
1B → 2B → 3B → home, with forced runners advancing if necessary.
Extra logic for sacrifice_fly, double_play, triple_play.
Errors and BIP outcomes may combine to allow “hit + error” or “single + runner scores”.
This table basically maps onto _resolve_ball_in_play.
