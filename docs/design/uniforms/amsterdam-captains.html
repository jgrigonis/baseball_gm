Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Orange, navy and cream are the same values the game uses for the Amsterdam badge.
  • Old-style capitals. Alegreya SC at its heaviest weight, in the spirit of Dutch Golden Age printing and sea charts.
  • XXX. The three St Andrew's crosses are the city's oldest mark, seen on its bollards, and here they run down the stirrups.
  • One symbol on the cap. The ship's wheel, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion, tower, wing, tulip, eagle, shamrock, star and dragon.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
