Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Magenta, navy and gold are the same values the game uses for the Hong Kong badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Neon. Righteous is a rounded sign face. On the road jersey a faint wide stroke behind the letters gives the glow of a neon tube.
  • Short words, wide margins. KINGS at home and on the alternate, with the crown above it, and a big HK on the road, all well inside the body.
  • Letters on the cap. HK in the neon face, the first cap in the set with letters instead of a symbol. The crown lives on the sleeve badge, the jersey chest and the stirrups.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
