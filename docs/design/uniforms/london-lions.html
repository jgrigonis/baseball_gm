London Lions

Pillar-box red, guardsman gold and the navy of a City pinstripe suit. The lettering is a heavy Victorian slab, straight off a music-hall poster. The cap carries a gold L, the alternate is a navy pinstripe like a banker's three-piece, and the patch is Tower Bridge at night with a gold crown above it.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Red, gold and navy are the same values the game uses for the London badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Music-hall type. Ultra is a fat Victorian slab, the face of old playbills and railway posters.
  • Short words, wide margins. LIONS at home and on the alternate, and LONDON on the road, all well inside the body.
  • Letters, not lions. A gold L on the caps, helmet and sleeves, and Tower Bridge with a crown on the patch.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
