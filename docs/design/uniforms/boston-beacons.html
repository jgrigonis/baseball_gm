Boston Beacons

Beacon gold, harbor night and lighthouse red. Boston Light, out on Little Brewster Island, was the first lighthouse in America (1716), and it is the team's logo. The lettering is Graduate, a varsity block face for a college town: BEACONS on white at home, BOSTON on the lighthouse-red road jersey, and a harbor-night Lantern alternate with a beam of light across the chest.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Beacon gold, harbor night and lighthouse red are the same values the game uses for the Boston badge: a gold badge with BOS in night and a red ring.
  • Not New York. New York's road jersey is navy, so Boston's dark color only shows up on the Lantern alternate, and the road jersey is lighthouse red, which no other North American team wears. The badge is gold, which no other North American team uses either. The nearest badges in the pool are Mumbai and Jakarta, on the other side of the world.
  • Display type. Graduate is a varsity block face, cut like the felt letters on a college sweater, for the city of Harvard, MIT and Boston College.
  • The lighthouse is the logo. The caps, batting helmet and sleeve badge all carry Boston Light with its two beams. The stirrups' red and white bands echo a lighthouse's day mark.
  • In the game. Boston is one of the extra teams, placed in North America east of New York by longitude.
