A runner on first base scores on a double roughly 21% of the 
time, according to OOTP Developments Forums analysis. While 
a runner on second scores on a single closer to 60% of the 
time, the risk of getting thrown out at home from first on a 
double is higher, often leading coaches to hold runners at 
third base. 

Success Rate: Despite the risk, when sending the runner, 
studies indicate a very high success rate of over 96% when 
a runner is sent home from first on a double, but teams are 
generally more cautious, allowing the runner to score only 
about 21% of the time.

Context: The decision often depends on the number of outs, 
the speed of the runner, and the location of the ball.

Alternatives: If the runner does not score, they are left 
on third base, which still provides a high probability of 
scoring with 0 or 1 out. 

Meaning, 0 or 1 out probably lowers likelihood of trying 
to score.
