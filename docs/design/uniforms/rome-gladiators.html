Rome Gladiators

Arena maroon, gladiator bronze and Colosseum travertine. All the lettering is Cinzel, cut from the Roman capitals on Trajan's Column. GLADIATORS at home, ROMA on the maroon road jersey, and a bronze alternate that says SPQR and wears its number in Roman numerals. The caps carry a gladiator's crested helmet, and the patch is the Colosseum at dusk.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Maroon, bronze and travertine are the same values the game uses for the Rome badge. The home body is a warmer, lighter travertine than the accent, so the stone color reads on grass.
  • Not London. London is already red and gold, so Rome goes darker: maroon instead of pillar-box red, and bronze, the metal of a gladiator's helmet, instead of gold. The badge says SPQR rather than ROM, the way Mexico City's says CDMX.
  • Display type. Cinzel is drawn from Roman inscriptional capitals. Its Roman numerals carry the alternate's number, XII, so the set has a jersey that could only be Rome's.
  • The helmet is the logo. The caps, batting helmet and sleeve badge all carry the same crested gladiator's helmet, facing forward to the right.
  • In the game. Rome is one of the extra teams, placed in Europe between Milan and Berlin by longitude.
