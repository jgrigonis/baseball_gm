Key Details on Passed Ball Stats

Consecutive Game Records: In 2022, Jacob Stallings set the MLB record 
with 228 consecutive games without a passed ball, passing Hall of 
Famer Johnny Bench’s 224-game record.

Total Pitches/Innings without a Passed Ball: In 2025, Cal Raleigh and 
Dillon Dingler accomplished the rare feat of catching over 1,000 
innings in a season without a single passed ball. Reports indicate 
Raleigh caught over 17,000 total pitches in 2025 without a passed ball.

Definition: A passed ball is charged when a catcher fails to hold or 
control a legally pitched ball that should have been held with 
"ordinary effort," allowing a runner to advance.

Distinction from Wild Pitches: If the pitch hits the ground or is 
deemed uncatchable, it is generally ruled a Wild Pitch (WP) rather 
than a Passed Ball (PB).
