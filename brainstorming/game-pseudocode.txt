taken ball:

taken strike:
    strike += 1 or strikeout()

swinging strike:
    strike += 1 or strikeout()

strike 3 dropped by catcher:
    batter runs if first base open or 2 outs

foul ball:
    check contact type. (foul tip, foul pop up, foul line drive, foul grounder)
    check location, only locations that matter are playable by 1st, 3rd, rf, lf, catcher, or rarely pitcher

foul tip:
    catcher could catch on 3rd strike
    otherwise it is regular_foul

foul pop up:
    chance for fielder to catch
    otherwise it is regular_foul

foul line drive:
    regular_foul

foul grounder:
    regular_foul

regular_foul():
    if less than 2 strikes, strikes += 1
    otherwise just a pitch count increment

fair_ball():
    check contact type. (pop up, line drive, grounder)
    check location and distance

foul_bunt():
    if less than 2 strikes, it is regular_foul

fair_bunt():
    basically a fair grounder short of the mound

bunt_popup:
    popup near catcher, pitcher, or another infielder
    might be caught, might be handled like a grounder

pickoff():
    could result in nothing at all. (runner goes back to base, safe)
    could result in runner picked off at the base
    could result in a run down between bases.

take_swing_bunt():
    if the take is on from the manager, take
    if bunt is on from the manager, bunt or take



take():
