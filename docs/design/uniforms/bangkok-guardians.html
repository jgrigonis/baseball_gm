Bangkok Guardians

Temple emerald, leaf gold and lacquer red, after the giant guardians that stand at the gates of Bangkok's temples. The lettering is Chonburi, a Thai display serif. The caps say BKK, the alternate carries กรุงเทพฯ, Krung Thep, the city's Thai name, and the patch shows the prangs of Wat Arun over the Chao Phraya.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Emerald, gold and red are the same values the game uses for the Bangkok badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Chonburi is a Thai display serif with Latin letters to match, so GUARDIANS and กรุงเทพฯ come from the same face.
  • Short words, wide margins. GUARDIANS at home, BANGKOK on the road, and กรุงเทพฯ on the alternate, all well inside the body.
  • Letters on the caps, the drawing on the patch. BKK carries the caps, helmet and sleeve badge. The temple only appears on the patch, where it has room.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
