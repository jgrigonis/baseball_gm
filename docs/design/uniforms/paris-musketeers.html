Paris Musketeers

The King's Musketeers wore a blue cassock with a silver cross and fleurs-de-lis. This set takes that blue and silver, adds a red trim, and letters it in a heavy Didone, the French poster face. The cap carries a silver fleur-de-lis, and the patch crosses three rapiers under the motto: tous pour un.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Blue, silver and red are the same values the game uses for the Paris badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Abril Fatface. A Didone, the high-contrast style the Didot family of Paris made famous. The wordmarks sit well inside the body, with room on both sides.
  • Silver on the road. The road jersey is silver-gray instead of blue, so the set has white, silver and blue jerseys.
  • One symbol on the cap. The fleur-de-lis, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion, tower, wing, tulip, eagle, shamrock, star, dragon, wheel and compass rose.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
