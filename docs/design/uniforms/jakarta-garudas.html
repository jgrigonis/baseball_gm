Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Gold, soga brown and red are the same values the game uses for the Jakarta badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Lilita One is a fat, friendly poster face that reads well at a distance.
  • Short words, wide margins. GARUDAS at home and on the alternate, and JAKARTA on the road, all well inside the body.
  • Garuda and batik. The garuda is drawn as its own bird, not the national emblem. Parang, the diagonal batik motif, runs across the alternate hem and the stirrups.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
