Uniform set · extra team

Berlin Bears

Black, red and gold, for the bear on Berlin's coat of arms. The lettering is Anton, a tall condensed poster face. BEARS at home, BERLIN on the road, and a red alternate that says BÄREN. The caps carry the standing bear, and the patch puts it in front of the TV tower.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Black, red and gold are the same values the game uses for the Berlin badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Anton is tall and condensed, like the posters on a Litfaßsäule. It handles the umlaut in BÄREN cleanly.
  • The bear is the logo. The caps, helmet, sleeve badge and patch all carry the same standing bear, upright with its tongue out, as on the city's arms.
  • In the game. Berlin is one of the extra teams for 32-team leagues.
