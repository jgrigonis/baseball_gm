Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Orange, purple and gold are the same values the game uses for the Shanghai badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Poller One is a wide, heavy poster face in the spirit of 1930s Bund signage.
  • Short words, wide margins. PHOENIX at home, SHANGHAI on the road, and a big SH on the alternate, all well inside the body.
  • The phoenix. The caps and helmet carry a phoenix with raised wings and a flame tail. SH stays on the sleeve badge, and the patch shows the wings behind the Pearl Tower.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
