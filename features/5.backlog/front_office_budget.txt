Front office budget

4 spending areas:

Scouting — accuracy of player grades. Low scouting budget means 
your grades have wider uncertainty (you might think a player is 
B+ when he's actually C+). High budget means tighter, more reliable 
grades. Matters most during the draft and when evaluating trade targets.

Coaching — rate at which players develop toward their talent caps. 
Better coaches push players harder toward their ceiling, especially 
young players. A team that invests here can develop mid-round draft 
picks into stars.

Player Development / Facilities — affects injury recovery speed 
and how well players hold up through a long season. Teams that 
skimp here see more wear-and-tear injuries and slower returns 
from the IL.

Analytics — how effectively your strategic profile translates 
to in-game decisions. A well-resourced analytics department means 
your "aggressive steal" setting actually runs at the right times; 
a poor one means suboptimal execution even with the right strategy set.
