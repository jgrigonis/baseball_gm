Traditional Fielding Statistics 

These are the standard stats found on a player's back-of-the-baseball-card 
profile: 
Putouts (PO): Credited to the fielder who physically records the 
out (e.g., catching a fly ball, tagging a runner, stepping on a base 
for a force play).

Assists (A): Awarded to fielders who touch the ball 
before a putout is recorded by another fielder.

Errors (E): Charged to a fielder who fails to make a play with "ordinary effort" 
that results in a batter/runner advancing or reaching base.

Total Chances (TC): The sum of Putouts, Assists, and Errors (PO + A + E). It represents 
the total opportunities a player had to make a play.

Fielding Percentage (FPCT or FP): The percentage of time a fielder makes a play without an 
error, calculated as: \((PO+A)/TC\).

Innings Played (INN): The total innings a player spent at a specific position.

Double Plays (DP) / Triple Plays (TP): Credited to a fielder when they are involved in 
a play where two or three outs are recorded.

Passed Balls (PB): Charged specifically to catchers when they fail to hold a pitch that 
they should have caught, allowing a runner to advance.

Outfield Assists (OFA): Specific assists recorded by outfielders, often when throwing out a 
runner. 

Modern & Advanced Defensive Metrics 

Advanced metrics aim to measure a player's range and impact on preventing 
runs, often relying on "Statcast" data (tracking technology): 

Outs Above Average (OAA): The current primary metric. It measures how many 
outs a player adds or misses compared to an average fielder based on catch 
probability (outfield) or distance/time (infield).

Defensive Runs Saved (DRS): A metric that calculates how many runs a player 
saved (positive) or allowed (negative) compared to an average player, 
factoring in range, errors, and throwing arm.

Ultimate Zone Rating (UZR): Measures a player's performance relative to the 
league average, considering range, errors, and double-play ability.

Range Factor (RF): A formulaic approach (usually \((PO+A)\times 9/\text{Innings}\)) 
to measure how many plays a fielder makes, which is generally more accurate than 
fielding percentage.

Fielding Run Value (FRV): Statcast's overall metric that converts a player's 
total defensive performance into a single run-based value.

Catch Probability: Used for outfielders to measure the likelihood of a fly 
ball being caught based on distance needed to cover and time available.

Pop Time (Catchers): The time it takes a catcher to get the ball from their 
glove to a fielder's glove on a stolen base attempt. 

Contextual Metrics 

Defensive Efficiency Ratio (DER): A team-level statistic representing the 
percentage of balls in play that the defense converts into outs.

Def (FanGraphs): A combined metric that adds fielding runs above average to 
positional adjustments to give an overall value of a player's defensive worth
