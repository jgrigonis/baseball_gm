
Closer: high strikes, high accuracy, high clutch. Stamina and recovery 
barely matter since they're throwing 1 inning max, often only in save situations.

Setup: similar profile but slightly lower ceiling, comes in the 7th-8th

Middle relief: more stamina matters here since they might eat 2-3 innings

Long relief: basically a starter skill profile — stamina is important, 
might come in early if the starter gets knocked out

So the bullpen selection algorithm becomes situational:

9th inning, save situation → closer

7th-8th, lead to protect → setup

Blowout or early exit → long relief first to save the good arms

Tie game late → best available by stuff, not role

The "stuff" score for a pitcher could just be a weighted composite:

stuff = (strikes * 0.5) + (accuracy * 0.3) + (clutch * 0.2)

Multiplied by get_stat_modifier() from fatigue so a tired closer
with great stuff loses out to a fresh setup man.
