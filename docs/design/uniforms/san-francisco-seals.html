San Francisco Seals

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Fog gray, red and charcoal are the same values the game uses for the San Francisco badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Bevan is a heavy old-style slab with a ballpark feel. The numbers use Rammetto One, because Bevan's numerals are short.
  • Short words, wide margins. SEALS at home and on the alternate, and a big S and F on the road, either side of the placket, all well inside the body.
  • Letters on the caps, the drawing on the patch. SF carries the caps, helmet and sleeve badge. The bridge only appears on the patch, where it has room.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
