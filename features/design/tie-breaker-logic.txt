Two-Club Tiebreaker Procedure
If two teams are tied, the following steps are taken in order until the tie is b
roken:
Head-to-head record: Best winning percentage in games between the two clubs.

Intradivision record: Best winning percentage in games within their own division.

Intraleague record: Best winning percentage in games against the same league 
(AL or NL).

Second-half Intraleague record: Best winning percentage in the second half 
of the season against the same league.

Second-half +1 Intraleague record: Continuing to add one game back from the 
second half until a winner is decided. 

Three-Club (or more) Tiebreaker Procedure
If three or more clubs are tied, the same steps are used, but with specific, 
in-group applications: 

Best Head-to-Head: The team with the best combined winning percentage against 
all other teams involved in the tie wins.

If still tied, the clubs are ranked by the best winning percentage in all 
intradivision games.

If two teams are still tied after the above, the two-club tiebreaker rules 
are applied. 

Wild Card and Seeding
These rules apply for division titles, Wild Card spots, and determining 
playoff seeding, ensuring a quick, mathematical resolution.
