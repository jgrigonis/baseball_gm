Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Ocean blue, slate and orange are the same values the game uses for the Sydney badge. I added white for the home body and the surf.
  • Surf poster type. Bowlby One is a heavy display face, like the lettering on an old surf-shop sign.
  • Short words, wide margins. SHARKS at home and on the alternate, and SYDNEY on the road, all well inside the body.
  • The fin. A dorsal fin cutting a wave on the caps, the helmet and the sleeve badge, and the wave again on the alternate hem and the stirrups.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
