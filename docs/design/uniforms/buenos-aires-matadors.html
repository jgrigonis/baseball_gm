Buenos Aires Matadors

The sky over the Río de la Plata and the suit of lights. Celeste, sun gold and the red of the muleta, lettered in an ornate wood type like an old bullring poster. The cap carries a gold BA, the alternate is a red traje de luces with gold embroidery, and the patch is the cape and sword under the sun.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Celeste, sun gold and red are the same values the game uses for the Buenos Aires badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Bullring poster type. Rye is an ornate wood type, like the letters on an old corrida poster.
  • Short words, wide margins. MATADORS at home and on the alternate, and a big BA on the road, all well inside the body.
  • Letters on the cap. A gold BA, like the HK in Hong Kong. The cape and sword live on the patch.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
