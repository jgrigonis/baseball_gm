Nearly perfect (radar/statcast/timing):

SP — 60-yard dash, statcast sprint speed, exact
AR — radar gun on throws, very precise
KK — spin rate, velocity, highly measurable
ST — pitch count data, innings logged, objective

Good but not perfect (large sample stats):

PO — exit velocity, hard contact rate, converges with enough ABs
CT — contact rate, K%, needs a season+ to stabilize
EY — walk rate, chase rate, needs sample size
GB — ground ball %, pretty reliable with enough pitches
AC — walk rate, command metrics, reliable over time

Soft/requires interpretation:

BR — baserunning runs above average, exists but noisy
RE — how quickly a pitcher recovers between starts, only visible over a 
full season and even then confounded by usage patterns
IF/OF — defensive metrics are notoriously noisy, years of data needed
CA — catching framing is measurable now but still debated
BU — rarely used enough to get a real read

Essentially invisible:

CL — the whole point is it's unknowable until the moment arrives
