Player Hapiness

Different players are driven by different factors.

Available factors are:

Fame - prefer large market teams
Location - similar to Fame, but they may prefer 
    certain cities/countries/climates/areas
Money - prefer high salary
Winning - prefer to play for a winning team
Impact - Prefer to be a star among their teammates
    Includes getting ample playing time.
Coaching Quality - prefer to be coached by the best
Stable Organization - prefer to pay for a team with a 
    proven track record

Preferred Teammates - some may prefer to play with some
    players and dislike others

Most likely won't implement all these any time soon.
