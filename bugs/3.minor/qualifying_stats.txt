Batting and Pitching stat qualifiers need adjustments


https://www.mlb.com/glossary/standard-stats/rate-stats-qualifiers

For relief pitchers:

0.5 IP for relief-pitcher specific reports

For fielding:

0.5 games for catchers; 0.67 games for infielders/outfielders; 
1.0 IP for pitchers unless a pitcher with fewer innings 
pitched has a better fielding percentage in more total chances


Rookies (MLB regular season)	1.55 PA	0.5 IP

Several MLB teams have operated without a "qualified" starting 
pitcher (minimum 162 innings) in recent seasons, a trend driven 
by injury prevention, the use of "openers," and analytics favoring 
shorter outings. As of 2022, 30% of MLB teams (9 of 30) did not 
have a single pitcher reach the ERA qualifying mark, illustrating 
a widespread shift away from traditional, high-volume starters.
