Thursday March 19th, 4pm

The league totals are clean across all 11 seasons — every core stat 
in range, stable year over year. ERA at the leader level is meaningfully 
better, with the best pitchers now regularly hitting 3.0-3.2 and the 
10th percentile tightening from 4.3 down toward 3.8 by season 11. Stolen 
bases are finally behaving — leaders are now regularly in the 30-38 range 
which touches the low end of expected. The career traces look excellent: 
Benjamin is a legitimate SP with consistent starts and sensible pitch 
counts, Anderson is used correctly as a closer with ~57 games and ~1 
IP each, Barnes is a proper short-relief closer.

Strikeouts are still stubbornly low at the leader level — the leader is 
consistently below 180 where 180 is the floor of expected. K/AB is still 
roughly double expected. These two move together and haven't moved despite 
all the other fixes, which confirms the problem is in pitch outcome 
probabilities rather than anything we've changed. This is the tuning 
problem from before.

Walks at the 10th percentile are still consistently below the expected 
floor in most seasons. The best passers are fine but the average qualifier 
still walks too few.

Sun March 15, 7pm

Leaders:

K/AB ↑↑ every single year (0.253-0.266 vs 0.125-0.150) — strikeout rate 
is still double the expected range. The KK coefficient reduction helped 
but didn't fully solve it

Walk leader ↑↑ consistently (91-129 vs 70-90) — there are always outlier 
pitchers walking far too many batters. The top of the distribution is 
broken even if the middle is fine

IP leader ↑↑ every year (250-264 vs 180-230) — no fatigue or innings 
limit system, elite pitchers just keep throwing


New observation:
SB leader flipped from ↑↑ (1.03-1.10/g) to ↓✓ (23-35 leaders vs 25-73 
expected). The stolen base fix may have slightly overcorrected — or 
the lower SB attempt rate just produces less variance at the top.

The K/AB and walk leader problems are almost certainly the same root 
cause — a small number of elite pitchers with very high KK and low AC 
are dominating the extremes of both distributions. One pitcher can lead 
in both strikeouts (257 in 2035) AND walks (129 in 2035) — that's a guy 
who misses a lot of bats but also can't find the zone. The IP leader 
problem compounds this: that same pitcher is throwing 263 innings. Fix 
the IP limiter first and the walk/strikeout outliers will naturally compress.

Totals:

AVG is extremely close — it's only 0.003-0.005 below the floor of 0.243. 
One season (2033) actually hits ✓ at 0.244. This is likely the in-play 
conversion issue we identified — singles coefficient slightly too weak. 
A small nudge to 3 * (contact - 50) → 3.5 * (contact - 50) in 
InPlayModifiers would probably close this gap.

Walks are persistently just over the ceiling — 3.41-3.53 vs ceiling of 
3.42. The accuracy coefficient is slightly over-generating balls. The 
walk leader outliers (91-129 BB) in the leader report are a separate 
elite-pitcher problem, but the population-wide walk rate just barely 
above ceiling suggests AC's ball suppression (-30 * (accuracy - 50)) 
might need a very small increase.

Assists consistently low is interesting and unchanged across all three 
sims. This is a fielding system issue — ground balls aren't generating 
enough assist opportunities. Either the GB rate in the engine is too low, 
or the fielding resolution isn't converting ground balls into assist 
chances at the right rate. Worth looking at separately.

HBP persistently low — 0.26-0.29 vs floor of 0.30. The HBP coefficient 
in the pitch modifier (-(accuracy - 50)) is probably too aggressive at 
suppressing hit-by-pitches for accurate pitchers.

Overall this is a much healthier sim than either previous version. The 
remaining issues are small calibration tweaks rather than structural problems.
