The persistent problems (every single season):
K/AB is ↑↑ every year without exception — 0.246-0.270 vs expected 0.125-0.150. 
This hasn't moved in 21 seasons. It's not drifting, it's structurally wrong. 
The strikeout rate is roughly double what it should be. This needs a direct 
intervention in the pitch outcome probabilities — either KK is too effective, 
the strike probability is too high, or contact is too suppressed in the resolver.

ERA 10th percentile is ↑ almost every year — 3.12-3.83 vs floor of 1.80. The 
league's worst qualifying starters are ERA 3.5+ consistently. This is healthy 
actually — the floor being above 3.0 means no truly dominant bottom-of-rotation 
pitchers, which is realistic. Not a problem.

Walks leader is ↑ most years — 83-111 vs ceiling of 90. One high-BB pitcher per 
league getting 100+ walks annually is the AC development lag showing. Improving
over time (111 in 2025 → 85-95 by 2040s) as AC matures — working as designed.

IP leader is ↑ most years — 225-258 vs ceiling of 230. Still not solved, though 
better than the previous run's 250-270. The fatigue cap should be helping but 
one or two relievers still eating 240+ innings.

Things that improved vs previous run:
Stolen bases are much better — previous run had SB persistently over ceiling, 
this run is mostly ✓✓ with occasional ↓. The 10th percentile at 21-26 is occasionally 
below the 25 floor but not dramatically. The base attempt probability adjustment worked.

HBP looks healthy throughout — 10-16 annually, consistently ✓✓. The AC suppression 
fix resolved the previous run's chronic undercount.
WHIP is mostly ✓ now vs previous run's consistent ↑ — AC development producing real 
results.

New concerns:
Triples are ↓ every single year — 5-9 vs expected 7-23. The floor is barely met and 
usually missed. Either SP-based triple generation is too low or the triple probability 
in the resolver needs a bump.

Sac bunts ↑ occasionally — 13-19 vs ceiling of 15, appearing in 2031, 2034, 2035, 2038, 
2040, 2041, 2044. The frequency is increasing over time which is odd. Possibly the 
bunting skill developing over time is pushing the probability above threshold in the late era.

ERA leader below floor in 2044 — 1.71 marked ↑↓ (below floor). One anomalous dominant 
season every 20 years is acceptable variance, not a systematic problem.

The K/AB issue is the single highest priority. Everything else is marginal or trending 
right. At 0.25-0.27 K/AB vs expected 0.125-0.150, batters are striking out at roughly 
2x the real rate. The question is whether the expected range itself needs updating 
for modern baseball — real 2024 MLB K/AB is around 0.225 — or whether the sim's strike 
probability is genuinely too high. Given the range was set at 0.125-0.150 which reflects 
1980s-era baseball, this might be a case where the expected range needs revision upward 
to 0.200-0.260 rather than the sim being broken.
