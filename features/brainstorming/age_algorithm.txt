
Each year, skills grow or increase, based on age of the player.

A new csv line can be generated for each player to go in a season
specific csv team file.

Injuries (not yet implemented) should be tracked furing the season
and should affect some skill growth/decrease.

We won't track birthdays exactly, every player just has one in the 
offseason. So, if you are 25 for one seaons, you are 26 in the next.
