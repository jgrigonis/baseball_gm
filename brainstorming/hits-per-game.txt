The average number of hits in an MLB game has hovered around 8.2 
to 8.4 per team in recent seasons (2022-2025), meaning a typical 
game sees about 16-17 total hits between both teams, though it 
fluctuates slightly yearly, with slightly fewer hits in 2024 (8.20) 
than 2023 (8.40). This reflects the overall offensive environment, 
where hits are generally plentiful, though strikeouts have increased 
in recent years, making the hit-to-strikeout ratio a notable trend.  

Key Figures: 
2025: ~8.26 hits per team (total ~16.5).
2024: ~8.20 hits per team (total ~16.4).
2023: ~8.40 hits per team (total ~16.8).
