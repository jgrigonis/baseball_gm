Vienna Maestros

A concert-hall look: plum like a velvet curtain, gold like the opera house trim, and cream like an old score. Every piece carries a hint of music, from the conductor's-baton patch to a five-line staff on the stirrups.

Plum #4B1D3F Gold #CBA135 Cream #F4EDE1

Jerseys

Front views. Home and away follow the league's usual cream and color split; the alternate is for Friday nights.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Plum, gold and cream are the same values the game already uses for the Vienna badge. The only additions are a near-black for Opera Night and a plum-gray for the away pants.
  • Script at home, block capitals on the road. The home set says the nickname in script; the road set says the city in serif capitals. That matches how most clubs split it.
  • One music idea per piece. The baton on the cap and patch, the staff on the stirrups. It stays a baseball uniform, not a costume.
  • Pinstripes at home. They're faint (about a fifth opacity) so the cream still reads as cream from a distance.
  • Gold is always outlined. Gold on cream gets weak, so gold pieces carry a plum or cream edge.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
