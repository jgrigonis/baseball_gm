Seattle Rain

Puget Sound navy, mist and evergreen teal, for the city's famous weather. Rain is set in Kaushan Script, a rising brush script, with SEATTLE and the numbers in Russo One. The caps carry the Space Needle, the helmet says SEATTLE, and the patch puts the Needle in the rain.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Navy, mist and teal are the same values the game uses for the Seattle badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Display type. Kaushan Script gives the short word Rain some motion. Russo One, a rounded sporty sans, carries SEATTLE and the numbers.
  • A singular name. The team is the Rain, so the jerseys say Rain, not a plural.
  • In the game. Seattle is one of the extra teams for 32-team leagues.
