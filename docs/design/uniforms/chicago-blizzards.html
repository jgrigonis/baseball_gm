Chicago Blizzards

Lake-effect winter on a baseball field. Navy like a January night, ice blue like the lake when it freezes, and white like fresh snow. The theme carries through in icicles hanging from the wordmark, a snowflake on every sleeve, and a jagged wind band on the stirrups.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Navy, ice blue and white are the same values the game uses for the Chicago badge. The only addition is a blue-leaning gray for the away pants.
  • Block letters, not script. Vienna got the flowing script; a team called the Blizzards gets heavy slab capitals that look like they could stand up to the wind.
  • Icicles only where there's a straight edge to hang from. They hang from the home and Lake Effect wordmarks. On the road, "CHICAGO" arches, so it goes without.
  • Ice blue is always outlined. It's pale enough to vanish against white, so it always carries a navy or white edge.
  • The patch is on the player's right sleeve, like Vienna's, which leaves the chest lettering room.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
