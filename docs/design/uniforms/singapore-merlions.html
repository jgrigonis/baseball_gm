Singapore Merlions

Half lion, half fish, all Lion City. Bright teal like Marina Bay at noon, red like the national colors, and cream like the old shophouses. The Merlion's fish half shows up as scales on the sleeves and stirrups, and the patch puts the bay's skyline over the water.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Teal, red and cream are the same values the game uses for the Singapore badge, plus a deeper teal for outlines and the belt.
  • The fish half is in the scales. The Merlion is hard to draw small, so its tail becomes texture on the sleeves and stirrups, and the skyline carries the city.
  • An arch at home this time. Vienna and Mumbai climb and Chicago is flat, so Singapore's home script arches like the road name.
  • One mark on the cap, no letters. Following the Mumbai anchor, the cap carries just the skyline.
  • Every word stays inside the seams. I measured each wordmark against the jersey body.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
What to tell me: keep, change, or drop each piece. Once Singapore is settled, I'll commit it and roll the next team.
