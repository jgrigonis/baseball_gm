In a full Major League Baseball (MLB) season, there are a total of 2,430 regular-season 
games played across all teams. 

This number is calculated based on the following factors:
There are 30 teams in the MLB.
Each team is scheduled to play 162 regular-season games.

The total number of games can be calculated by multiplying the number of teams by the 
number of games per team and dividing by two (since two teams play in every game): 
(30 teams x 162 games) / 2 = 2,430 games. 

This total excludes any potential tie-breaker games or the games played during the 
postseason playoffs and World Series.

1961 to present has had a 162 game season
1904-1960 had 154 game season.
