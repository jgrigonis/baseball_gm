Uniform set · extra team

Auckland Kiwis

All Black, fern green and silver, the colors New Zealand wears on the world stage. The logo is the silver fern, New Zealand's sporting emblem, a single frond curling at the tip. The lettering is Titan One, a round, heavy poster face: KIWIS on white at home, TĀMAKI, the city's Māori name, on the all-black road jersey, and AUCKLAND on a green Silver Fern alternate.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. All Black, fern green and silver are the values the game uses for the Auckland badge: a black badge with AKL in green and a silver ring.
  • Not another Frankfurt or Berlin. Both of those are black with red. Auckland pairs black with fern green and silver instead, the colors of the silver fern, so the badges are easy to tell apart. The nearest badges in the pool are Frankfurt, New York and Vienna, all well clear.
  • Display type. Titan One is a round, heavy poster face that suits a round, heavy bird, and it has the macron for TĀMAKI on the road jersey.
  • The silver fern is the logo. The caps, helmet and sleeve badge carry a single silver fern frond, tilted a little with its tip curling over. The same fern runs up the stirrups and sweeps across the alternate, and the name and the patch carry the Kiwis.
  • In the game. Auckland is one of the extra teams, placed in Oceania east of Sydney by longitude, so Oceania has two teams.
