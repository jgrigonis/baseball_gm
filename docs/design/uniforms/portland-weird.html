Portland Weird

Forest green, rose red and a loud chartreuse, for a city that asks to be kept weird. Everything is lettered in Shrikhand, a fat retro display face. Weird at home with every letter at its own tilt, the PDX airport carpet on the road, and a rainbow alternate. Every cap and the helmet say PDX, and the patch puts a red rose in front of Mount Hood.

Design notes

  • New team colors. Portland had no colors yet, so I added forest, rose red and chartreuse to the game's color table, with POR for the badge. I added cream for the home body.
  • Display type. Shrikhand is fat, bouncy and a little odd, which fits. The letters of Weird and PDX are each tilted a different way.
  • The carpet. The road jersey borrows the teal PDX airport carpet and its runway-map pattern, the one people photograph their feet on.
  • A singular name. The team is the Weird, so the jerseys say Weird, not a plural.
  • In the game. Portland is one of the extra teams for 32-team leagues.
