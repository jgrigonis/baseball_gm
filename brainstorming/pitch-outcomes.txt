Key Notes for _resolve_pitch

_resolve_pitch should only mutate the count and generate PA-ending events if applicable.
Anything that moves runners, scores, or changes field state goes to _resolve_ball_in_play.
PlateAppearanceEndEvent is the sole mechanism for ending a plate appearance.
_resolve_ball_in_play should consume RNG to determine hit type (single/double/etc.) and run advancement.
Tests can now be deterministic by controlling RNG and counting events.
