Tokyo Samurai

Indigo like a dyed hakama, the vermilion of a shrine gate, and the dull gold of old armor fittings. The lettering is a heavy Japanese gothic. The cap carries a gold kabuto with its horned crest, the alternate wears 侍 across the chest, and the patch crosses two katana in front of the red sun, with 東京.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Indigo, vermilion and gold are the same values the game uses for the Tokyo badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Heavy gothic. Dela Gothic One covers both the Latin letters and the kanji, so 侍 and 東京 match the wordmarks.
  • Short words, wide margins. SAMURAI at home, TOKYO on the road and a big 侍 on the alternate, all well inside the body.
  • The kabuto. A samurai helmet with the horned kuwagata crest, on the caps, the batting helmet, the sleeve badge and the stirrups.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
