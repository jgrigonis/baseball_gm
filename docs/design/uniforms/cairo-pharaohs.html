Cairo Pharaohs

Lapis, gold and carnelian, the stones of a pharaoh's treasure. The logo is a pharaoh in the striped nemes headcloth, and the stripes run down the road jersey's sleeves too. The lettering is Limelight, an Art Deco face from the Egyptian revival that followed the opening of Tutankhamun's tomb: PHARAOHS on papyrus at home, CAIRO on the gold road jersey, and a lapis alternate with a broad bead collar and القاهرة in El Messiri.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Lapis, pharaoh gold and carnelian are the same values the game uses for the Cairo badge: a lapis badge with CAI in gold and a carnelian ring.
  • Not Istanbul. Istanbul, the nearest team, wears cobalt on the road with turquoise, so Cairo's road jersey is gold and its lapis is a lighter, warmer blue. No other West Asia team has a blue badge with gold letters; the nearest badges in the pool are Los Angeles and Vienna.
  • Display type. Limelight is an Art Deco face in the spirit of the 1920s Egyptomania after Tutankhamun's tomb was found. El Messiri, used for القاهرة, was drawn by an Egyptian type designer.
  • The pharaoh is the logo. The caps, helmet and sleeve badge carry a pharaoh's head in the striped nemes, with the cobra on his brow and the false beard. He is kept small on the caps and helmet.
  • In the game. Cairo is one of the extra teams, placed in West Asia next to Istanbul by longitude.
