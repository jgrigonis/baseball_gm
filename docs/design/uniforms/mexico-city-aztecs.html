Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Jade, obsidian and gold are the same values the game uses for the Mexico City badge. I added white for the home body.
  • Market sign type. Bungee is a blocky sign face, a nod to the hand-painted rótulos on the city's stalls and shopfronts.
  • Short words, wide margins. AZTECS at home and on the alternate, and CDMX on the road, all well inside the body.
  • The sun and the fret. An eight-rayed gold sun on the jade cap and the sleeve badges, and the stepped fret of the temple friezes on the alternate and the stirrups.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
