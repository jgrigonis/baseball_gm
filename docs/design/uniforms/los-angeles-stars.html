Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Purple, gold and pink are the same values the game uses for the Los Angeles badge. I added white for the home body and an orange that only appears where gold fades into pink.
  • Script at 10°. Pacifico, a sign-painter's script, on all three jerseys. The numbers are in Bungee, a marquee face.
  • One symbol on the cap. The star, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion, tower, wing, tulip, eagle and shamrock.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
