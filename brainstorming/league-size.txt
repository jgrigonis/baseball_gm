Major League Baseball (MLB) has had 30 teams since the start of the 1998 season. This 
means there have been 30 teams for over 27 years. 
The Arizona Diamondbacks and the Tampa Bay Devil Rays (now the Tampa Bay Rays) were 
the 29th and 30th franchises added to the league during the 1998 expansion. 
This period is the longest stretch in MLB history without an expansion since the period 
before 1961 when the league only had 16 teams. The league is currently considering 
expanding to 32 teams in the future.
