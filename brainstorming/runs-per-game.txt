MLB's average runs per game fluctuates yearly but generally hovers 
around 4.3 to 4.6 runs per team, with recent years showing around 
4.39 (2024) to 4.62 (2023) runs per team, reflecting trends in 
offense and pitching. 

Recent Trends (Runs Per Game per Team) 
2024: ~4.39
2023: ~4.62
2022: ~4.28
2021: ~4.53

Key Factors Influencing Runs Per Game

Offense vs. Pitching: Higher run totals often correlate with more 
home runs, while lower totals can indicate dominant pitching or 
larger ballparks. 

Rule Changes: Adjustments like the pitch clock can speed up games 
and sometimes affect run scoring. 

Era Differences: The game's scoring has changed dramatically; the 
high-scoring 1990s saw nearly 5 runs per game, while the "Year of 
the Pitcher" in 1968 dropped to under 3.5.
