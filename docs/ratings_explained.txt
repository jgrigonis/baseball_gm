Name - Self Explanatory, the name of the player

In the __init__ file of the player class, all the templates are stored
for each position. These are used to create a random player based on 
the specified postion.

PS - This is just a position that the player was created with. Any player
can play any position, some won't even be best suited for the position
they were created with.

** Attributes **
T - Throws. This is the hand the player throws with (r, l)

B - Bats. This is the side of the plate the player bats from, right, 
left or switch (r, l, s)

CL - Clutch. Barely implemented yet, but this is intended to determine 
how "clutch" a player is 

SP - Running Speed. Very useful for running bases, outfielder range, 
and infield range as well.

PO - Power Hitting. How hard can the player hit the ball.

EY - Batting Eye. How selective can the player be in terms of taking 
bad pitches.

CT - Contact Hitting. How effective is the player at making contact 
with pitches.

BR - Base Running. How smart is the player on the bases. Smarter base 
runners can steal easier, and won't get picked off as easily. Also, they
won't make base running mistakes like trying to overextend.

BU - Bunting Skill. How well can the player can lay down a bunt 

CA - Specific Catcher skills, like blocking wild pitches, and throwing out 
base stealers. 

IF - Infield specific skills, cleanly fielding ground balls, etc.

OF - Outfield skills, such as tracking fly balls, playing hops and caroms

AR - Throwing Arm strength. Determines accuracy and speed of throwing the
ball.

** Pitching Attributes **
AC - Pitching Accuracy. How accurately can a pitcher hit a spot. Will reduce
balls and wild pitches.

ST - Pitching Stamina. A pitcher's energy will go down slower with higher 
stamina rating.

RE - Pitching Recovery. A pitcher's energy will recover faster with rest with
a higher recovery rating.

GB - Ground Ball Pitching. How effective a pitcher is at getting ground balls
intead of flies.

KK - Pitching Strikes. How effective the pitcher is at making batters miss their
pitches.

** Positional Ratings **
P - Pitching Rating. Overall pitching rating, based on pitching attributes.
Average of Accuracy, Stamina, Recovery, Ground Balls, Strikes and Clutch

C - Catcher Rating. Positional rating based on catching skills and some others.
Average of 5 x Catching, Arm and Clutch

1B - First Baseman Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.
Average of 5 x Infielding and Clutch

2B - Second Baseman Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.
Average of 5 x Infielding, Running Speed and Clutch

3B - Third Baseman Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.
Average of 5 x Infielding, Arm and Clutch

SS - Short Stop Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.
Average of 5 x Infielding, Running Speed, Arm and Clutch

LF - Left Field Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.
Average of 5 x Outfielding, Arm, Speed, and Clutch

CF - Center Field Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.
Average of 5 x Outfielding, 2 x Arm, 3 x Speed, and Clutch

RF - Right Field Rating. Positional rating based on relevant skills.  
Average of 5 x Outfielding, 2 x Arm, Speed, and Clutch

** Hitting Ratings **
LO - Lead Off Hitter Rating. Relevant hitting skills for a lead off hitter.
Average of Running Speed, Eye x 2, Contact x 2, Base Running and Clutch

CH - Contact Hitter Rating. Relevant hitting skills for a contact hitter.
Average of Eye x 2, Contact x 4, and Clutch

PH - Power Hitter Rating. Relevant hitting skills for a power hitter.
Average of Power x 4, Eye, Contact and Clutch
