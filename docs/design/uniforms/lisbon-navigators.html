Lisbon Navigators

The Age of Discovery, sailing out of the Tagus. Sand like the limestone of Belém, navy like the open Atlantic, and the red cross that flew on the caravels' sails. The lettering is a soft, heavy serif, the cap carries a compass rose, and the patch is a caravel under full sail.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Sand, navy and red are the same values the game uses for the Lisbon badge.
  • Fraunces. A heavy serif with soft, ink-trap curves, in the spirit of old Portuguese charts and printing.
  • LISBOA on the road. Like Warsaw and Beijing, the road jersey uses the city's own name.
  • One symbol on the cap. The compass rose, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion, tower, wing, tulip, eagle, shamrock, star, dragon and wheel.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
