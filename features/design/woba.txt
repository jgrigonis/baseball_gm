Weighted On-Base Average (wOBA) is a comprehensive sabermetric 
statistic that measures a hitter's overall offensive contribution 
per plate appearance by assigning weighted values to different 
ways of reaching base (e.g., HR > 3B > 2B > 1B > BB). It is scaled 
to resemble on-base percentage (OBP), with a league average typically 
around 0.330, offering a more accurate measure of run production than 
OPS or batting average.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WOBA

Typical wOBA Values
.400+: Elite/MVP Level
.370+: All-Star Level
.320: Average
<.300: Below Average
