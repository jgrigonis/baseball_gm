Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Green, orange and white are the same values the game uses for the Dublin badge. I added a dark green for edges and shadows.
  • Uncial. The rounded capitals of early Irish manuscripts. Uncial Antiqua is wide, so "Shamrocks" is arched and kept at a modest size.
  • Two languages. The patch gives the city in English and in Irish.
  • One symbol on the cap. The shamrock, following the anchor, spire, falcon, morion, tower, wing, tulip and eagle.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
