Seoul White Tigers

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Cream, black and blue are the same values the game uses for the Seoul badge. The blue is the blue of the taegeuk.
  • The white tiger. Baekho, the white tiger of the west, drawn in the flat, wide-eyed way of the old folk paintings, with blue eyes. It's on the caps, the helmet, the sleeve badge and the patch.
  • Short words, wide margins. TIGERS at home, SEOUL on the road and 서울 on the alternate, all well inside the body. Black Han Sans covers both the Latin and the Hangul.
  • Stripes. The alternate's cream sleeves and both pairs of stirrups carry black tiger stripes.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
