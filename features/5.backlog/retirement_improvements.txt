A cap-aware retirement system would be a meaningful upgrade. 
The inputs are all there:

Headroom score — sum of remaining cap space on relevant skills 
weighted by position. A 32-year-old OF with OF headroom of 0, CT 
headroom of 0, EY headroom of 2 has essentially zero development ceiling.

Decline trajectory — how many key stats are already in decline phases 
vs growth phases.

Age — as a modifier, not the primary signal.
