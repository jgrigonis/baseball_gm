Madrid Conquistadors

Old Castile, iron and gold. Iron gray like a morion helmet, gold and red like the Spanish flag, and cream like the stone of the Plaza Mayor. The nickname is in Castilian blackletter, the city in carved Roman capitals, the cap carries the crested helmet, and the patch is a shield with crossed Toledo swords.

Design notes

  • Built from the team's three colors. Iron, gold and red are the same values the game uses for the Madrid badge. I added a cream for the home body, since none of the three is light enough.
  • A long name, arched. Conquistadors is 13 letters, so the nickname is in a narrower blackletter, and MADRID and the patch keep the Roman capitals.
  • Red sleeves on both main jerseys. They tie the cream home and the iron road jerseys to the flag colors.
  • One symbol on the cap. The morion, following the anchor, spire and falcon.
  • In the game. These are design drawings only for now. Nothing in the app has changed.
